Revision control

BOQ import

Automatic column mapping

Preview

Engineering material calculator

Dimensions are interpreted in mm. The density table is editable. The calculator supports plate, bar, rod, pipe/tube, sheet, cylinder and custom volume.

Supplier comparison

Record supplier quotations and choose one supplier/price per material. Alibaba search is provided for live sourcing; automated commercial retrieval should be connected through an approved API/backend.

MPP manufacturing cost

Complete Excel-model cost build-up

This V5 preserves the identified workbook relationships: raw material + casting → 7% material overhead → direct labour → machining → service → factory overhead → head-office overhead → total manufacturing cost → profit → selling price. The detailed engineering sheets are represented as editable inputs below.


Drawings & part files

Upload one or more drawing files (PDF, JPG, PNG) for a part. Fill in the sheet metadata (sheet number, title, weight, scale, overall size, sheet size). The drawing will be shown when you click the "View" button in the Parts table.

Revision files

Keep each BOQ, MPP and drawing attached to its revision. Use immutable revision numbers for approved engineering baselines.

Supabase connection

Use your project's Supabase URL and publishable/anon key. Never put a service-role key in this browser app. Supabase recommends protecting exposed tables with Row Level Security.
